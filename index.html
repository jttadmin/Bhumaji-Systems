Cloud Services · 04

Make cloud spend visible, explainable and efficient.

Cloud costs grow when resources are oversized, idle, poorly tagged or not aligned with usage. We help you understand where spend goes and prioritize changes that reduce waste without compromising reliability.

01

Understand spend

Review billing trends, service-level costs, tags, environments and the main cost drivers.

02

Right-size resources

Identify over-provisioned compute, unattached storage, idle resources and mismatched capacity.

03

Improve architecture

Evaluate autoscaling, storage tiers, managed services and workload scheduling where appropriate.

04

Set guardrails

Configure budgets, alerts, ownership tags and review routines to catch unexpected spend earlier.

01 · BaselineCapture current spend and usage patterns.
02 · PrioritizeRank opportunities by impact, risk and effort.
03 · ImplementApply agreed changes with validation and rollback.
04 · TrackCompare results and establish ongoing reviews.
What you receiveA cost review, prioritized optimization backlog, budget and tagging recommendations, and a repeatable review cadence.
Our approachRecommendations are workload-specific. We do not promise fixed savings before reviewing actual usage and constraints.

About Bhumaji Systems

Practical cloud engineering for businesses that want to grow with confidence.

Bhumaji Systems is a cloud and DevOps engineering services company focused on helping startups, small and medium-sized businesses, and software agencies build, modernize and operate dependable technology platforms. We turn complex infrastructure requirements into practical, secure and maintainable solutions that support real business goals.

WHO WE ARE

A hands-on engineering partner

We work alongside your team to plan cloud environments, automate delivery workflows and improve production operations. Our focus is on clear technical decisions, sensible architecture and solutions your team can understand, maintain and extend—not complexity for its own sake.

WHAT WE DO

From cloud foundations to production

Our work includes AWS cloud setup and migration, infrastructure as code with Terraform, container platforms with Docker and Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, GitOps delivery, monitoring and observability, security practices, backup and disaster recovery, and cloud cost optimization. We can help with a focused task or a broader improvement initiative.

WHO WE HELP

Teams at different stages

For startups and small businesses, we help establish a secure cloud foundation without unnecessary spend. For software agencies, we can provide engineering support for client projects, migrations, deployment automation and platform operations. For teams already in production, we help identify reliability, delivery and cost improvements.

HOW WE WORK

Clear, collaborative and outcome-led

We start by understanding your goals, current environment, constraints and risks. Then we agree on scope and priorities, implement in manageable steps, validate the result and document important operational details. We value transparent communication, repeatable automation, secure defaults and knowledge sharing throughout delivery.
