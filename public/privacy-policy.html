Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Bhumaji Systems respects your privacy. This page explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when you visit bhumaji.com or contact us through the website.

Information you provide

If you contact us or submit an enquiry, we may receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, business email address, company, phone number and enquiry message. We use these details to respond to your request and communicate about relevant services.

Cookies and browser storage

This website currently does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The cookie-preference control may store your choice in your browser's local storage so that the banner does not appear on every visit. This preference is not sent to Bhumaji Systems. Essential website functionality may also rely on standard browser or hosting technologies.

You can change your choice using the “Cookie Settings” link in the website footer. If analytics, advertising or other optional tracking technologies are added in the future, this policy and the consent controls should be updated before those technologies are enabled where consent is required.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses a custom domain. Hosting providers and network infrastructure may process standard technical information, such as requests and security logs, to deliver and protect the website. For details about provider practices, please consult the relevant provider's privacy documentation.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information you submit may be handled by service providers needed to host the website or deliver communications. We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to submit personal information through the website. To ask a question about this policy or request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, contact info@bhumaji.com.

This policy is general website information and is not legal advice. Please review it against your actual data practices and applicable requirements before relying on it as a compliance document.
